## 话题：电影与娱乐 | Movies & entertainment

### Q1. Are you a movie person? Do you watch a lot of films?

1. Whatever you end up doing, love it the way you loved the projection booth when you were a little squirt.
   - 中文：不管你最后去做什么，都要用你小时候爱那间放映室的方式去爱它。
   - 词火：whatever you end up doing 不管你最后做什么 / love it the way you loved 像当初爱…那样去爱 / a little squirt 小屁孩（亲昵） / end up doing 最终做了
   - 出处：《天堂电影院》Cinema Paradiso（1988），Alfredo 台词（轻微改编：原为两句 "Whatever you end up doing, love it. The way you loved the projection booth when you were a little squirt."，合并为一句）

2. I'd never call myself a film buff, but a good movie at the end of a hard day works better for me than anything else.
   - 中文：我可不敢自称影迷，但辛苦一天结束时，一部好片子对我比什么都管用。
   - 词火：call oneself a film buff 自称影迷 / at the end of a hard day 辛苦一天结束时 / works better for me than 对我比…更管用 / anything else 别的任何东西
   - 出处：AI 仿写（非真实出处）

### Q2. Why do you think people keep going to the movies?

1. If you've ever wondered where your dreams come from, you look around... this is where they're made.
   - 中文：你要是好奇过梦是从哪儿来的，看看周围——它们就是在这儿被造出来的。
   - 词火：if you've ever wondered 如果你曾好奇过 / where your dreams come from 梦从哪儿来 / look around 看看四周 / this is where they're made 它们就是在这里诞生的
   - 出处：《雨果》Hugo（2011），Georges Méliès 台词（原句）

2. Of all the arts, movies are the most powerful aid to empathy, and good ones make us into better people.
   - 中文：在所有艺术里，电影最能帮人长出同理心，而好电影会把我们变成更好的人。
   - 词火：of all the arts 在所有艺术中 / the most powerful aid to 对…最有力的助推 / aid to empathy 同理心的助力 / make us into better people 让我们成为更好的人
   - 出处：Roger Ebert（影评人，2012 年好莱坞星光大道致辞时期的著名表述）（原句）

### Q3. Do you prefer watching movies at home or in a cinema?

1. I just met a wonderful new man, and he's fictional, but you can't have everything.
   - 中文：我刚认识了一个特别好的新男人，他虽然是虚构的，但人嘛，不可能什么都占全。
   - 词火：a wonderful new man 一个特别好的新男人 / he's fictional 他是虚构的 / you can't have everything 人不能什么都占全 / just met 刚认识
   - 出处：《开罗紫玫瑰》The Purple Rose of Cairo（1985），Cecilia 台词（轻微改编：原为感叹号断句 "I just met a wonderful new man! He's fictional, but you can't have everything."，改为逗号连接以凑成长句）

2. I still think a cinema beats my couch every time, because nobody pauses the movie to check their phone in the dark.
   - 中文：我还是觉得电影院每次都胜过我家沙发，因为在黑灯里没人会暂停电影去看手机。
   - 词火：beat my couch 胜过我窝在沙发上 / every time 每次都 / pause the movie 暂停电影 / check their phone 看手机
   - 出处：AI 仿写（非真实出处）

### Q4. What kind of movies do you usually go for?

1. We don't read and write poetry because it's cute. We read and write poetry because we are members of the human race.
   - 中文：我们读诗写诗不是因为它讨巧可爱，而是因为我们是人类的一员。
   - 词火：read and write poetry 读诗写诗 / because it's cute 因为它可爱讨巧 / members of the human race 人类的一分子 / we don't... because 我们不是因为…才…

2. People will want to go to it because you're passionate about it, and people love what other people are passionate about.

### Q5. Has a movie ever really moved you or changed something for you?

1. The only true currency in this bankrupt world is what you share with someone else when you're uncool.

   - 词火：the only true currency 唯一真正的通货 / in this bankrupt world 在这个破产的世界里 / share with someone else 与他人分享 / when you're uncool 当你不那么体面的时候

2. There are maybe three movies that changed how I see my own family, and I still rewatch one of them every couple of years.
   - 中文：大概有三部电影改变了我看自家人的方式，其中一部我每隔几年还会重看一遍。
   - 词火：change how I see 改变我看待…的方式 / my own family 我自己的家人 / rewatch 重看 / every couple of years 每隔几年
   - 出处：AI 仿写（非真实出处）

### Q6. Do you think movies and TV shape how people see real life?

1. We've all been raised on television to believe that one day we'd all be millionaires, and movie gods, and rock stars.
   - 中文：我们都是被电视喂大的，被教着相信有一天我们都会成为百万富翁、银幕之神和摇滚明星。

2. I don't think a movie turns anyone into a bad person, but it definitely tells you what a normal life is supposed to look like.
   - 中文：我不觉得一部电影能把人变坏，但它确实在告诉你，所谓正常人生应该长什么样。
   - 词火：turn someone into 把某人变成 / a bad person 坏人 / it definitely tells you 它确实在告诉你 / what a normal life is supposed to look like 正常生活该是什么样子

## 话题：健康与运动 | Health & exercise

### Q1. Do you exercise regularly?

1. You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems.
   - 中文：你不会自动升到目标的高度，你只会掉到你自己那套系统的水平。
   - 词火：rise to the level of 上升到…的水平 / your goals 你的目标 / fall to the level of 掉到…的水平 / your systems 你日常那套系统
   - 出处：James Clear《原子习惯》Atomic Habits（2018）（原句）

2. I'm not the person who runs at six in the morning, but I do walk forty minutes every single day, rain or shine.
   - 中文：我不是那种早上六点去跑步的人，但我确实每天都走四十分钟，风雨无阻。
   - 词火：the person who runs 会去跑步的那种人 / at six in the morning 早上六点 / every single day 每一天 / rain or shine 风雨无阻
   - 出处：AI 仿写（非真实出处）

### Q2. Why do so many people quit their workout routine?

1. The purpose of setting goals is to win the game. The purpose of building systems is to continue playing the game.
   - 中文：定目标是为了赢下这一局，建系统是为了能一直玩下去。
   - 词火：the purpose of setting goals 定目标的意义 / win the game 赢下这一局 / building systems 搭建系统 / continue playing the game 继续玩下去
   - 出处：James Clear《原子习惯》Atomic Habits（2018）（原句）

2. Most people quit because they picked a plan that only works when life is calm, and life is almost never calm.
   - 中文：大多数人放弃，是因为他们挑的那套计划只有在生活风平浪静时才管用，而生活几乎从不风平浪静。
   - 词火：most people quit 大多数人放弃 / picked a plan 挑了一套计划 / only works when 只在…时才管用 / life is almost never calm 生活几乎从不安稳
   - 出处：AI 仿写（非真实出处）

### Q3. What keeps you going when training gets really hard?

1. You, me, or nobody is gonna hit as hard as life, but it ain't about how hard you hit. It's about how hard you can get hit and keep moving forward.
   - 中文：你、我、任何人，都不会出手比生活更重，但重点不是你能打多重，而是你能挨多重还继续往前走。
   - 词火：hit as hard as life 打得跟生活一样狠 / it ain't about how hard you hit 关键不在你打得多狠 / get hit 挨打、受挫 / keep moving forward 继续往前走
   - 出处：《洛奇 6：永远的拳王》Rocky Balboa（2006），Rocky 对儿子的台词（轻微改编：原为多句，删去末尾重复句 "How much you can take and keep moving forward"，并把前两句用 but 连为一句）

2. It's supposed to be hard. If it wasn't hard, everyone would do it. The hard is what makes it great.
   - 中文：它本来就该难。要是不难，人人都能干。正是这份难，才让它了不起。
   - 词火：it's supposed to be hard 它本来就該难 / if it wasn't hard 要是不难 / everyone would do it 人人都会去做 / the hard is what makes it great 正是难成就了它
   - 出处：《红粉联盟》A League of Their Own（1992），Jimmy Dugan 台词（原句）

### Q4. Do you pay attention to what you eat?

1. Every action you take is a vote for the type of person you wish to become.
   - 中文：你做的每一件事，都是在给你想成为的那种人投一票。

### Q5. How do you deal with stress and bad weeks?
